# Graceway Baptist Church — Website Design Ideas

## Three Approaches

### Approach A: Sacred Depth
Dark Grace Navy backgrounds with luminous gold accents, layered depth through subtle textures and soft glows. Probability: 0.07

### Approach B: Warm Sanctuary
Light Cloud (#F4F6F7) backgrounds with Grace Navy typography, warm photography-forward layout. Probability: 0.06

### Approach C: Modern Reverence ← CHOSEN
Clean, editorial layout combining Grace Navy hero sections with light content areas. Asymmetric grid, generous whitespace, serif display type paired with clean sans-serif body. Probability: 0.08

---

## Chosen Approach: Modern Reverence

**Design Movement:** Contemporary Sacred — editorial magazine meets traditional church warmth

**Core Principles:**
1. Grace Navy (#17324D) anchors authority; Cloud (#F4F6F7) provides breathing room
2. Warm Gold (#C9973E) used sparingly as a sacred accent, never decoratively
3. Asymmetric layouts break the "centered church website" cliché
4. Photography-first storytelling — real community moments over stock imagery

**Color Philosophy:**
- Background: alternating Grace Navy and Cloud sections create rhythm
- Primary text on light: Charcoal (#242A30)
- Primary text on dark: White (#FFFFFF)
- Links/interactive: Way Blue (#2F6F9F)
- Accent: Warm Gold (#C9973E) — dividers, icons, CTAs

**Layout Paradigm:**
- Hero: full-viewport with diagonal overlay, left-aligned text
- Content sections: alternating left/right asymmetric grid
- Cards: subtle shadow, no heavy borders
- Navigation: sticky top nav, transparent → opaque on scroll

**Signature Elements:**
1. Thin gold horizontal rule as section divider
2. Script 'G' monogram from v3 template as recurring brand motif
3. Diagonal section cuts between Navy and Cloud backgrounds

**Interaction Philosophy:**
- Smooth scroll with subtle entrance animations (fade-up, 200–300ms)
- Nav transitions from transparent to Navy on scroll
- Hover states: gold underline on links, subtle lift on cards

**Animation:**
- Hero text: staggered fade-up, 80ms per line
- Section entrances: fade-up with 20px translate, 250ms ease-out
- Nav: 200ms opacity transition on scroll
- Buttons: scale(0.97) on active, 160ms ease-out

**Typography System:**
- Display/Hero: Source Serif 4 (Google Fonts), Bold 700
- Headings: Source Serif 4, SemiBold 600
- Body/UI: Inter, Regular 400 / Medium 500
- Korean: Noto Sans KR

**Brand Essence:** A welcoming Baptist community where grace meets everyday life — for seekers, believers, and families.
Adjectives: Warm, Trustworthy, Alive

**Brand Voice:** Direct, warm, scripture-grounded. Headlines invite rather than announce.
Example lines: "Grace is not a destination — it's the way." / "Every Sunday, a seat is waiting for you."

**Signature Brand Color:** Grace Navy #17324D

## Style Decisions
- Hero section uses diagonal clip-path to transition into Cloud background
- Gold rule (1px, #C9973E) used as section separator and nav accent
- Scripture verse appears as styled blockquote in multiple sections
- Korean bilingual text appears in footer and official documents section
- Photography: prioritize real Graceway community moments with documentary warmth; avoid generic cathedral/worship stock unless treated as secondary atmospheric layer
- Layout: every major page must include at least one asymmetric editorial composition; ministry/listing pages should avoid uniform card grids unless broken by a featured story or offset visual rhythm
- Signature motifs: thin Warm Gold rule, diagonal navy/cloud transition, and script 'G' monogram must appear as recurring structural brand signals on every primary page
- Ministries page: redesign with featured ministry hero + alternating asymmetric rows instead of uniform 2-col card grid
- Home hero: add diagonal clip-path bottom transition to cloud section
- Section headings: move away from centered layout toward left-aligned editorial style with gold accent rule
